SubverseLab Tester Agreement

Version 1.1 · 14 October 2026 · Confidentiality, testing and licence terms

This agreement is between Senol Sahan, trading as SubverseLab (subverselab.com, "SubverseLab", "we") and the person who accepts it ("Tester", "you"). It applies from the date you accept it as described in section 13.

1. Purpose

You help SubverseLab test its plug-ins, applications, online tools and models, and report problems and ideas. In return you receive the benefits in section 4. This is a voluntary collaboration, not employment, and no salary or fee is paid.

2. Definitions

  • Released Products: SubverseLab plug-ins and packages that are publicly available for sale or download.
  • Test Builds: any software, plug-in, model, preset, sample, installer or file that SubverseLab has not publicly released, and any version of a Released Product given to you before its public release.
  • Confidential Information: Test Builds; unreleased product names, features, sounds, screenshots, recordings, measurements, manuals, prices, release dates and plans; the contents of your tester account; and anything we mark or describe as confidential. Information that is already public through no fault of yours, or that you lawfully knew before we gave it to you, is not Confidential Information.
  • Tester Account: your personal account on subverselab.com, opened with the e-mail address you applied with.

3. Your role

  1. You test in your own production setup and report bugs, compatibility problems and improvement ideas through the report area of your Tester Account (or by e-mail if we ask).
  2. A useful report names the product and version, your operating system and version, your DAW and version, what you did and what happened. Screenshots or short videos help.
  3. You may suggest new online tools or plug-ins at any time.
  4. There is no minimum number of reports, but if you send no report for 90 days in a row we may move your account to inactive (section 9.1).

4. What you receive

  1. Tester Account: a dedicated area on subverselab.com with your downloads and a report form.
  2. Released Products: free use of SubverseLab's Released Products in your own music production, including commercial releases of your own music.
  3. Test Builds: when we need unreleased products or models tested, we place them only in the test area of your Tester Account. Each Test Build is for you alone.
  4. Products you help develop: when a product you tested as a Test Build is publicly released, you receive a licence to it on the same terms as section 4.2.
  5. Online tool credits: 2,000 credits per calendar month for the online tools on subverselab.com. Unused credits do not carry over, have no cash value and cannot be transferred.

5. Licence

  1. The licences in section 4 are personal, non-exclusive, non-transferable and non-sublicensable. They are perpetual (they do not end when your active testing ends), except that SubverseLab may revoke them under section 9.2.
  2. You may not sell, rent, share, give away, upload, resell or otherwise make available any product, Test Build, installer, licence key or account access to anyone else.
  3. You may not reverse engineer, decompile, disassemble, remove or bypass any protection, watermark or identifier, except to the extent the law expressly allows this despite this agreement.
  4. All intellectual property in the products, Test Builds, models and website stays with SubverseLab. You receive only the licence described here.

6. Confidentiality

  1. You keep all Confidential Information secret and use it only for testing for SubverseLab.
  2. You do not share, publish, stream, post or describe Test Builds or Confidential Information (including screenshots, audio, video, reviews or benchmarks) on social media, forums, websites or with any other person, unless SubverseLab gives written permission first.
  3. You take reasonable care to keep Test Builds and your account safe, and you tell us at once if you learn of any leak or unauthorised access.
  4. If you work with or test for other audio software companies, you tell us, and you never pass our Confidential Information to them.
  5. This section continues for three (3) years after your participation ends, and for each unreleased item until SubverseLab releases it publicly, whichever is later.
  6. AI assistants: using AI assistants or agents (for example ChatGPT) to write, structure, translate or proofread your reports is not a breach of this section.

7. Feedback

Reports, ideas and suggestions you send ("Feedback") may be used, changed and built on by SubverseLab freely, in any product, without payment or credit to you. You give SubverseLab a worldwide, perpetual, irrevocable, royalty-free right to use Feedback, and you confirm you have the right to give it. We may credit testers by name only with their permission.

8. Test Builds are unfinished

Test Builds may contain errors, crash, or damage projects or data. Work on copies and keep backups. Test Builds and the online tools are provided "as is", without any warranty. To the extent the law allows, SubverseLab is not liable for loss of data, projects, income or other indirect damage arising from testing.

9. Ending the agreement

  1. By either side: you or SubverseLab may end active testing at any time by e-mail. Licences already received under section 4 then stay in place, subject to section 9.2. Monthly credits and new Test Builds stop.
  2. For breach: if you knowingly and deliberately copy, share, resell or distribute any product, Test Build or licence illegally or against section 5, or leak Confidential Information or an unreleased model, SubverseLab may end this agreement immediately by e-mail. In that case all licences granted to you under this agreement, including the perpetual ones, are revoked, your Tester Account is closed, and you must delete every copy of every product and Test Build you received.
  3. Honest mistakes: small or accidental mistakes are overlooked. If something is shared by accident, tell us and help remove it; no further step follows.
  4. Sections 5.2 to 5.4, 6, 7, 8, 10 and 11 continue after this agreement ends.

10. Responsibility for breach

SubverseLab takes legal action only against intentional sabotage: when a tester knowingly, deliberately and persistently acts to undermine SubverseLab's business, for example by illegally copying, distributing or leaking products, Test Builds or models. Only in that case is the tester responsible for the damage this causes SubverseLab, including lost sales and reasonable legal and enforcement costs, and SubverseLab may take legal action, including civil claims, injunctions and criminal complaints under applicable intellectual property and copyright law (in Türkiye, among others, Law No. 5846 on Intellectual and Artistic Works). Each Test Build may carry an identifier unique to your account, which may be used as evidence of where a copy came from.

11. Law and courts

Before any formal step, both sides first try to resolve a disagreement by direct, good-faith discussion. If you live in the United States, any legal proceedings are handled by the competent courts of the U.S. state where you live, under the law that applies there. For testers living elsewhere, this agreement is governed by the laws of the Republic of Türkiye and the courts and enforcement offices of Istanbul (Central) have jurisdiction, without preventing either side from seeking urgent relief in the country where the tester lives.

12. Personal data

SubverseLab processes your name, e-mail address, application details (system, DAWs), reports and account activity only to run the tester programme, as described in the privacy policy at subverselab.com/privacy. You may ask us to see, correct or delete your data at any time by e-mail; deleting it ends your participation.

13. Acceptance

  1. You confirm you are at least 18 years old and free to enter into this agreement.
  2. You accept this agreement by replying, from the e-mail address you applied with, with the words: "I have read and accept the SubverseLab Tester Agreement v1.1." The date and time of that e-mail is the date of this agreement. SubverseLab may later ask you to confirm acceptance again on the website.
  3. Version 1.1 replaces version 1.0. SubverseLab may update this agreement for the future. We will send you the new version by e-mail; if you do not accept it, you may end active testing under section 9.1.
  4. This document is the whole agreement on testing between you and SubverseLab. If one part is found invalid, the rest stays in force.
