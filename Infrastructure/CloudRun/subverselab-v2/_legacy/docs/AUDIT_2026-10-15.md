### Root cause

The light theme itself is designed well. Every core colour is a token, and the dark theme only redefines tokens. The failures come from three gaps:

1. **The always-dark plug-in hero borrows page tokens.** `.plugin-hero` defines its own `--pl-*` palette. Buttons inside it still use the page's `.btn-outline` / `.btn-primary`, and those flip to ink in light mode. The worst failure (1.16) is the single-plugin buy button on every family room.
2. **`--pl-*` variables are used outside their scope.** `.plugin-btn` outside the hero has no background. A link rule (`.plugin-section a`) recolours anchor-buttons. Both are specificity and scope bugs that appear in either theme.
3. **The muted token sits just under AA on surfaces, and opacity stacks on top of it.**
   - `--color-text-muted` passes on the page background but not on `--color-surface` / `-surface-2`. In light: 4.37 and 4.01.
   - Extra `opacity` (0.55–0.85) on already-muted text pushes it to 2.6–3.6.
   - Fixing the two muted tokens and removing the opacities clears roughly 120 of the 142 light failures.

Not a cause: no product images or cards with dark backgrounds sit on light pages in a way that breaks text.

### Not verified / caveats

- In several full-page captures the card images (TUTKAL, ELEK, ADALET, SEDA, the free cards, the family image) and the video posters appear **blank**. This is a headless artefact of `loading="lazy"` with a fast scroll: `/plugins/tutkal/front_glue.png` returns 200 with 442 KB. The Phase 2 script should wait for the images.
- Contrast over photos and `url()` backgrounds was not measured (none were found on text). Hover and focus states were not measured.
- Headless only. The real 1920×1080 display was not checked, and the signed-in states were not rendered.
