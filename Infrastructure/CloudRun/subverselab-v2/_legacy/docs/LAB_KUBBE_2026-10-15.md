## What the prototype does
- **Hear it first.** One play button, four buttons: Dry / Hammam / Cistern / Valley (keys 0–3, space to play). All four renders
  run in sync, so switching keeps the position — you hear the room change mid-phrase.
- **Colour world per room.** The hero turns warm marble for Hammam, deep water for Cistern, dusk blue for Valley, grey for Dry.
- **The display moves.** The T60 readout glides with the plug-ins' own knob glide (30 % per 30 Hz tick); a rolling energy strip
  (one bar per 20 ms, like KUBBE's decay display) reacts to the audio; Valley marks its first echoes (32, 86, 201, 353, 469 ms).
- **Voice.** "One phrase. Three rooms. Switch while it plays." One line per room instead of a paragraph.
- Everything below the hero is the normal room (`PluginRoom` with a `hero` slot), so buying, manual and controls are unchanged.
