# FAQ

Generated from `knowledge/products.json`. Answers are readable out of the preset
files; anything that is not, is not answered here.

**Do I need Max for Live?**
No. Every device in every rack is stock Ableton.

**Do I need any third-party plugins?**
No.

**Which Ableton version do I need?**
Percussion Repeat was built in Ableton Live 12.4.5, Drum Buss in Ableton Live 12.4.3.
A rack will not open in a Live older than the one that saved it.

**What is actually in Percussion Repeat?**
EQ Eight → Compressor → Saturator → Beat Repeat → Redux → Reverb → Utility — 8 macros, 4 saved variations.

**What is actually in Drum Buss?**
EQ Eight → Glue Compressor → Saturator → Utility — 8 macros, 3 saved variations.

**What is a macro variation?**
A saved position of all eight macros at once. Switching between them gives you a
different treatment of the same material without touching a knob.

**Will it work on my track?**
Unknown, and anyone who tells you otherwise is selling. Try it.

**Where do I put the file?**
Anywhere in Live's browser, or your User Library. Then drag it onto an audio track.
